# Code of Conduct

We want the SmallPict community to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Good behavior includes:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults or personal attacks
- sexualized language or imagery
- publishing other people's private information without permission
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all smallPict repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to [support@smallpict.app](mailto:support@smallpict.app). All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
